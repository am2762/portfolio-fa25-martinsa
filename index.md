## About Me


![Profile Picture]({{ "assets/images/profile-pic.jpg" | relative_url }}){: class="profile-image"}

 
Aniket Martins is a senior at Cornell University, where he majors in mechanical engineering in the Duffield College of Engineering. A native of Fairfield, Connecticut, Mr. Martins is an Air Force ROTC cadet who commands i5 Space, the U.S. Space Force’s undergraduate space education and training organization, and will commission as a Space Force officer after graduation. A Boren Scholar, he has studied Mandarin at National Cheng Kung University in Taiwan. Mr. Martins is a research assistant in the Aerospace ADVERSARY Lab, a Junior Fellow at the Brooks Tech Policy Institute, a Meinig Family Cornell National Scholar, and a Laidlaw Scholar. His interests include autonomous systems, space security, and Indo-Pacific policy. He received the Space Force Association’s 2026 Guardian Scholarship.
